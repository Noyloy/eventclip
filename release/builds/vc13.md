## Root cause / regression evidence
- A 32 s selected-method run of HELD vc11 on S23 went PLANNING→EXPORT_FAILED in about one second with an empty reasonCode because start() swallowed the exception (fixed in vc12). A later S23 run of HELD vc12 named the throw: IllegalArgumentException: Failed requirement. from ProductMuxWatchdog.apply, called from ProductNativeComposition.start.
- Media3 transformer 1.5.1 Transformer clinit sets DEFAULT_MAX_DELAY_BETWEEN_MUXER_SAMPLES_MS with Util.isRunningOnEmulator() ? 21000L : 10000L. The product require that 21000==DEFAULT passes on the software emulator and fails immediately on a physical S23. That is the entire ~1 s S23 failure and why the emu 32 s test could not catch it. This require was introduced with ProductMuxWatchdog in vc11 (not in public vc10). The earlier Owner-reported ~32 s export failure on vc10 remains BUG-005 (AAC EOS pad), already fixed in this tree.
- Fix: keep setMaxDelayBetweenMuxerSamplesMs(21000) on both surfaces; drop the equality require; still require 21000>0 and 21000!=TIME_UNSET. Do not fall through to the 10s device default, do not raise the value, do not pass TIME_UNSET.
- Catching test: ProductMuxWatchdogTest#applySets21000WhenMedia3DeviceDefaultIs10000 reads Transformer.DEFAULT on the host JVM (10000, same as a device) and asserts apply() succeeds and writes 21000. The old require would have thrown. An emu-only apply()-succeeds test would not have caught this.

## The short version
EventClip makes Birthday-mode movies from your own photos, videos and songs, **entirely on your phone**. We don't collect, store, or share any of your data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads.

## What the app uses, and how
- **Photos and videos you pick.** You choose them with Android's built-in photo picker. EventClip only sees the items you select, not your whole gallery. They're used on your phone to build your movie.
- **Songs on your phone.** With your permission (Android's "Music and audio" access), EventClip lists audio files already on your device so you can choose a soundtrack. Only DRM-free audio files can be used. Song files are never copied off your phone.
- **Your projects and finished movies** are saved only on your phone. Rendering happens offline on the device.
- **Original sound** from your videos is kept, with music lowered under voices. All of that audio processing happens on the device too.

## What we do NOT do
- We don't collect or receive any personal information, photos, videos, audio, contacts, location or device identifiers.
- The app makes no network calls of its own. We don't include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- We don't sell or share data with anyone, because we never have it.

## Children
EventClip is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect data from children. Parents who let a child use the app should know that everything stays on the device.

## Your control
- You can revoke the music/audio permission at any time in Android Settings.
- Deleting a project in the app, or uninstalling EventClip, removes its projects from your phone. Movies you exported or saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.
- Since we hold no data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

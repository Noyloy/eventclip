## Full description: EN
Make a birthday movie in minutes, from your own photos, videos and music.

Pick the moments, choose a song already on your phone, and EventClip automatically cuts your clips to the music. Real voices stay in: music is lowered under people talking, so the laughs and the "happy birthday!" still come through.

**How it works**
• Choose photos and videos with Android's photo picker. EventClip sees only what you select.
• Pick a song from your phone's music (DRM-free audio files).
• Birthday mode auto-cuts everything into a movie.
• Export and keep it on your phone.

**Private by design**
• Everything is made offline, right on your device.
• No account, no sign-up.
• Your projects stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

**Free and Premium**
• Free: 3 exports per week, with a small watermark.
• Premium: $4.99/month or $29.99/year, billed through Google Play. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

**Languages:** English and Hebrew (full right-to-left support).
